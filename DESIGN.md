# DESIGN.md

## KOYMOBI Website Design System

### 1. Design Direction

Create a modern corporate website for KOYMOBI, a digital publishing and advertising monetization company.

The website should feel like a combination of:

* Digital Publisher
* AdTech company
* Mobile technology company
* Modern media business

The design should communicate:

**Professional
Reliable
Global
Technology-driven
Clean
Modern**

Avoid making the website look like:

* A marketing agency
* A crypto company
* A SaaS dashboard
* A generic WordPress business template
* An overly futuristic AI startup

---

# 2. Visual Style

Use a clean modern editorial-tech aesthetic.

Large typography.

Generous whitespace.

Strong visual hierarchy.

Minimal decorative elements.

Use cards sparingly.

Sections should feel spacious and premium.

Avoid excessive rounded cards everywhere.

Avoid excessive gradients.

Avoid excessive shadows.

# 25. Approved Visual Calibration — Yieldbird Reference

Approved on 2026-09-03 as an explicit operator preference. Use the reference only as visual inspiration; all KOYMOBI layouts, graphics, copy and assets remain original.

## Revised identity

Energetic, editorial, confident, global, approachable and technology-driven. Keep the site credible and clear rather than playful or agency-like.

## Revised tokens

* Background: `#FFFFFF`
* Soft blush surface: `#FFF6F8`
* Strong blush surface: `#FCE8ED`
* Primary text / dark sections: `#0C120C`
* Secondary text: `#475467`
* Primary coral: `#F2385A`
* Primary coral dark: `#CF1846`
* Supporting mint: `#20A46B`
* Supporting sky: `#2DADE0`
* Border: `#EADDE0`
* Default radius: 20px; CTA buttons may use a fully rounded silhouette.

## Revised layout and components

* Use oversized, heavy headlines with one short coral-highlighted phrase.
* Use broad white space, asymmetric two-column compositions and large colored section bands.
* Enrich abstract product visuals with floating labels, mini performance cards, interface rows and connected nodes.
* Alternate white, blush, coral and near-black sections to create a stronger page rhythm.
* Allow larger rounded panels and floating cards, but avoid turning every content block into a card.
* Do not add case studies, customer logos, testimonials or invented statistics.

## Current logo selection

Option A — editorial coral K symbol with split-color KOYMOBI wordmark. Selected provisionally by the operator. Matching favicon and social-preview mark are active; alternative concepts remain under `assets/logo-options/` for later comparison.

## Section transition refinement

The operator requested that all eyebrow labels (short line plus uppercase category text) be removed. Section transitions now use soft rounded overlaps, restrained shadows and gradual white / blush / near-black background changes inspired by the pacing of the approved reference site. Mobile uses smaller radii and shallower overlaps.

## Hero originality refinement

The hero headline uses a large primary line for “Owned Digital Media.” and a smaller coral secondary line for “Built for sustainable growth.” The former concentric-orbit ecosystem graphic and floating optimization labels were removed. The replacement is an original operating-model panel that explains the sequence from owned Web and Mobile products, through KOYMOBI operations, to diversified advertising demand.

## Content architecture calibration

The operator approved `https://www.quillmarkmedia.com/` as a business-content reference while explicitly retaining KOYMOBI's existing visual system. The reference informed the emphasis on owned-and-operated Web and Mobile products, consistent operating principles, clear supply positioning and partnership-focused calls to action. All KOYMOBI copy is original. Reference-site metrics, certifications, team biographies, locations, partner claims and compliance claims are excluded unless independently confirmed by the operator.
