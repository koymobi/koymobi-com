Cookie Notice

How cookies and similar technologies are used across the KOYMOBI corporate website.

Last updated: 4 May 2026. This notice explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on the corporate website at koymobi.com.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. We also use similar technologies, including local storage and pixel tags. We collectively refer to these as “cookies”.

Categories of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required to deliver the site and remember your privacy choices. These cannot be disabled.

Performance & analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These run only with your consent.

Marketing

Allow us to measure the performance of campaigns referring traffic to this site. These run only with your consent.

Cookies on properties we operate

Individual websites and applications operated by KOYMOBI may use additional cookies for advertising and audience measurement. The cookies and vendors used are described and managed through the consent management platform on each property.

Managing your choices

You can review and change your choices at any time through the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer of each property, or by clearing cookies in your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may limit your ability to use parts of the site.
