A practical four-stage process keeps commercial and technical expectations aligned.

01

Introduction

Share your company, platform, demand sources, priority markets and proposed relationship.

02

Confidential review

Where appropriate, both sides can establish confidentiality before exchanging detailed portfolio or performance information.

03

Technical fit

Teams align on formats, connection method, response timing, quality controls and reporting.

04

Test and refine

A suitable integration can begin with a controlled scope, followed by validation and gradual expansion.

Common partner questions.

These answers describe our intended partnership approach; exact implementation details are confirmed during evaluation.

Why are specific property domains not listed publicly?

KOYMOBI can share relevant product information with qualified partners in an appropriate business context. This keeps public corporate messaging focused while supporting detailed supply review.

How do you approach traffic quality?

We review acquisition patterns, engagement signals and partner reporting, and investigate unusual activity at the property level.

Which privacy and consent requirements do you support?

Requirements depend on the product, market, data use and integration. Applicable consent and privacy expectations are reviewed before launch.

Do you support private or direct programmatic arrangements?

Potential deal structures can be discussed based on inventory, technical capability, demand fit and commercial terms.

How is reporting reconciled?

Reporting sources, dimensions, cadence and discrepancy handling are agreed as part of technical onboarding.
